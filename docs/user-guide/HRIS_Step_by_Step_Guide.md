# HRIS Step-by-Step Guide

This guide explains the main HRIS workflows for administrators and employees using the live Docker environment.

## Step 1: Open the HRIS sign-in page

Start at the login page. Enter your HRIS username and password, then click Sign in to open the platform.

![Step 1](images\01-login-page.png)

## Step 2: Review the dashboard command center

The dashboard summarizes employee activity, pending tasks, leave SLA, company updates, and quick actions so HR can work from one place.

![Step 2](images\02-admin-dashboard.png)

## Step 3: Open the employee directory

Use Employees to review the master list, open employee records, and manage HR profile data from one organized table.

![Step 3](images\03-employee-directory.png)

## Step 4: Monitor attendance records

Attendance shows processed daily logs for review. HR can spot missing records, late arrivals, and incomplete time-ins or time-outs.

![Step 4](images\04-attendance-monitoring.png)

## Step 5: Manage leave requests

The leave workspace tracks balances and requests so managers and HR can review, approve, or reject submissions with full context.

![Step 5](images\05-leave-workspace.png)

## Step 6: Analyze HR reports

Reports combines employee, attendance, leave, and payroll summaries. Filters at the top help narrow the data before exporting or reviewing trends.

![Step 6](images\06-reports-overview.png)

## Step 7: Manage user accounts

User Access is where admins create accounts, reset passwords, toggle active status, and open per-user menu assignment controls.

![Step 7](images\07-user-access.png)

## Step 8: Review governance and approval queues

Access Governance centralizes access templates, menu requests, profile update approvals, dormant access checks, and audit history.

![Step 8](images\08-access-governance.png)

## Step 9: Check the notification center

Notifications collects leave alerts, access reviews, payroll events, and profile update decisions so actions are easy to track.

![Step 9](images\09-notifications.png)

## Step 10: Employee view of attendance logs

Employees can open My Attendance to review their own time-in and time-out history without seeing other staff records.

![Step 10](images\10-employee-attendance.png)

## Step 11: Employee leave request form

Employees can file leave requests by choosing a leave type, date range, and reason. The request then enters the approval flow.

![Step 11](images\11-employee-leave-request.png)

## Step 12: Employee self-service profile update

Employees can request updates for safe personal fields such as birthdate, civil status, address, and emergency contact. The changes stay pending until HR verifies them.

![Step 12](images\12-employee-profile-update.png)
